# Shipping and delivery

## Track your order
You can track any order from the My Orders page. Open the order and select Track to see the latest courier scan and the delivery estimate shown there. Order status updates as the courier scans the package.

## Delivery estimates
The delivery date shown at checkout and on the My Orders page is an estimate and can change if the courier reports a delay. Tracking updates appear as soon as the courier scans the parcel.
